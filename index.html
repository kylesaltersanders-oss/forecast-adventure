    How the lights are set

    Skydiving, student limits

    Australian Parachute Federation rules cap students at 15 knots of ground wind (Operational Regulation 6.4.2) and require the target to stay visible with no descent through cloud (6.4.1). Go needs 10 knots or less, under 35% low cloud and a dry day. Between 10 and 15 knots is a maybe, because the forecast has no gusts and the limit applies to them. Certificate A and B holders get 20 knots. The drop zone safety officer always has the final call.

    Kiteboarding

    Go needs 15 to 30 knots in the afternoon at Botany Bay. 12 to 14 knots is a maybe. The spot follows the wind direction: Kurnell for north through north-east and for westerlies, Kyeemagh or Dolls Point for easterlies, Dolls Point, Ramsgate or Wanda for south and south-east. Winds off the land are marked maybe because they are gusty. Check local kiting zones before launching.

    Climbing

    Sport crags with plenty at grade 20 and under: Berowra and Mt Ku-ring-gai in Sydney's north, Shipley Upper and Bardens Lookout in the Blue Mountains. Sandstone loses strength when wet, so 2 mm or more on the day, or 10 mm or more the day before, is a no. Lighter rain, 30°C or hotter, or under 10°C is a maybe.

    The climbing section's top pick is the best-lit climb, with your closest single-pitch target first, then Claude's fixed recommendations, then the rest of your targets. Multi-pitch and crags with no route picked come last.

    Tennis

    The fallback. Go needs a dry day under 17 knots and under 33°C in Sydney. It becomes the best pick whenever no adventure sport is a go.

    Mutch Park, Pagewood: 6 courts, $30 an hour, open 7 am to 10 pm by pin code. See today's courts and book

    Heffron Park, Maroubra: 10 courts, $35 an hour off-peak and $42 peak, open from 7 am to 10 pm Monday to Thursday, 9 pm Friday and 8 pm weekends. Book a court

    The forecast

    MET Norway's global model at six points, read at 8 am, 11 am, 2 pm and 5 pm for the first two to three days and at 11 am and 5 pm after that. It gives average wind, not gusts, and coastal sea breezes often run a few knots above it. Days six and seven are an early look only. Confirm on WillyWeather before you drive.
